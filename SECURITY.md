## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Chromaticc, you **CAN** open a public issue but might be a late response, if your looking for a quick fix. Instead, report it privately via one of these channels:

- Discord: DM a moderator (chrxmeelst) on the [Chromaticc server](https://discord.gg/chrxmaticc)
- Email: [chrxmaticc@proton.me](mailto:chrxmaticc@proton.me)

We will acknowledge your report within 48 to 72 hours and provide an estimated timeline for a fix. You will be credited for the discovery once the fix is released.

Thank you for helping keep Chromaticc safe!
