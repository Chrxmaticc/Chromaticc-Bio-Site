FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you might be wondering about wsp.baby.

What is wsp.baby?

A link-in-bio page. You get one page at wsp.baby/you for your socials, projects and anything else you want people to find.

How do I get in?

Two ways: get an invite code from a friend who's a member, or buy an invite for $5 on the Get Access page.

How often do members get invites?

Invites go out to members in occasional waves. They're scarce and not on a schedule, so if you don't know anyone with a code, buying one is the sure way in.

What about Premium?

Premium isn't included with an invite. You can get it separately in the Shop: custom domains, animated backgrounds, your own fonts and more.

Is the $5 a subscription?

No. It's a one-time payment for one invite. Nothing renews.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. See our Refund Policy for details.
